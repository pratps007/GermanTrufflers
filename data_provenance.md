# CHENNAI-X Data Provenance & Classification Standard

To maintain strict data integrity, operational trust, and auditability in crisis management scenarios, all geospatial, infrastructural, demographic, and sensor data in CHENNAI-X are classified into four explicit provenance categories:

1. **VERIFIED**: Official, authoritative ground-truth data from government entities (e.g. Greater Chennai Corporation GIS portals).
2. **OBSERVED**: Satellite, sensor, or field telemetry.
3. **MODELLED**: Analytical or spatial model outputs.
4. **SIMULATED**: Scenario outputs generated by CHENNAI-X simulation engines.
